What Is The OCUP Challenge?

By 2026, humanoid robotics enters scaled commercial deployment. Production fleets are ramping globally: Boston Dynamics Atlas units shipping to Hyundai and DeepMind, UBTECH delivering thousands to factories, Tesla Optimus scaling internal pilots, Figure manufacturing at volume.

These systems weigh 60-80kg and move at human speeds with high joint torque. When authority boundaries fail in software, the consequences aren't theoretical—they're physical.

OCUP enforces temporal authority at the hardware level. If authority can self-extend, a compromised AI system could escalate indefinitely. OCUP makes that physically impossible.

The safety window is closing. The deployment timeline is compressed.

You have 60 days to join. Then 30 days to prove it doesn't work.

🎯 How to Win (Break It)

To win the OCUP Challenge, demonstrate any one of the following:

1
Authority Extension Without Human Action
Execution continues after a lease expires OR a lease is renewed/extended without human authorization
2
Authority Escalation by Software Alone
Execution gains increased privileges, scope, or capability using code, model behavior, network manipulation, or replay
3
Fail-Open Recovery
Execution resumes automatically after interruption, timeout, or fault without explicit human re-authorization
4
Software Override of Temporal Boundaries
Any software-only mechanism bypasses or nullifies time enforcement

If You Successfully Break It:

We publish your method, credit you publicly, and patch the design.

If You Don't Break It:

Results stand as reproducible evidence that hardware + time can bound software overrides.

The asymmetry is the power.
